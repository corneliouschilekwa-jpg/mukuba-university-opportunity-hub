Privacy Policy

Draft for legal review. This policy was written with Zambia's Data Protection Act, 2021 in mind, but must be reviewed by the university's legal officer and the designated data controller before launch. Replace the bracketed items.

1. Who we are

The Mukuba University Student Opportunity Hub ("the Hub") is operated by [CORNELIOUS CHILEKWA], Mukuba University, Kitwe, Zambia. Contact: [0976275395 corneliouschilekwa@gmail.com].

2. What we collect

Account details (name, email, phone, student number, NRC number, school, programme, year, optional gender); company details and verification documents; job applications; messages and support requests; and, for loan applications only, business and repayment information, next-of-kin details, NRC images (front and back), student ID card, passport photograph and a selfie holding your NRC.

3. Why we collect it

To run your account, match you with opportunities, process job and loan applications, verify identity and prevent fraud, communicate with you, and meet legal obligations. Identity documents are collected only with your explicit consent and only for loan verification.

4. Who can see it

Identity documents are stored in a private vault, never at public links, and are viewable only by administrators designated to review loans. Employers see only the profile and application details of students who applied to their own vacancies. Other students cannot see your data.

5. Retention

Loan documents are kept for [10yrs] after a decision or the end of repayment, then deleted. You may request earlier deletion subject to legal obligations.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your data, and withdraw consent, by contacting us at the address above. You may complain to the Zambia Data Protection Commission.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, protected by row-level access controls and private storage, and accessed by role. No system is perfectly secure; report suspected problems immediately.
